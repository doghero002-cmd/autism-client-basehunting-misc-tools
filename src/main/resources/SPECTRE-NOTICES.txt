The ports adapt Donut and Base Raid modules, their required detection/helpers,
Spawner Nametags, Fake Stats and Region Map to the native AUTISM addon API.
No Spectre client framework, branding UI, updater, telemetry, Discord integration,
or separate Spectre runtime dependency is included. Existing local rendering,
settings, finder reports and inventory/navigation coordination are reused.

Modifications include native lifecycle and packet handling, client-thread queues,
bounded caches and scans, cleanup on world changes/disconnect, preserved addon
identifiers where replacing a module, native GUI/HUD drawing, corrected map
bounds/projection and integration with the addon's local fake payment balance.
Fake Stats draws a private scoreboard projection instead of replacing the live
client scoreboard, so other modules continue reading genuine server values.
